### 👋 About me

I'm an **electrical engineer at heart** who writes the firmware too. I design and wire the hardware,
bring up the microcontroller, and keep going until the instrument reads correctly on the bench.

Day to day I build and maintain **test and measurement equipment for motor-vehicle inspection** —
brake, side-slip, speedometer, axle-play, headlight, and exhaust-emission testers — as well as
**automated jig testers for consumer-electronics production lines**.

I'm also studying **Electrical Engineering at the Faculty of Electronics and Computer Engineering
(FTEK), Universitas Kristen Satya Wacana**.

**Focus areas:** power & signal wiring · sensor interfacing & signal conditioning · STM32 / ESP32
firmware · industrial instrumentation · test automation · PCB bring-up

### 🔧 What I work on

| Area | What I do | Stack |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vehicle test equipment** | Electrical design, sensor interfacing, and firmware for brake, side-slip, speedometer, axle-play and headlight testers used in roadworthiness inspection | STM32, ESP32, load cells, speed sensors |
| **Exhaust-emission bench** | Reverse-engineering an NDIR gas-analyzer module, designing the bench wiring, and planning the driver/display stack | NDIR, UART, power distribution |
| **Production jig testers** | Automated pass/fail jigs for audio boards on consumer-electronics production lines | Microcontrollers, signal measurement |
| **Brake-tester computerization** | Research on retrofitting legacy mechanical brake testers with digital acquisition and PC software | Sensors, data acquisition, desktop UI |
| **Toolchain & workflow** | Reproducible build/flash setups: Arduino CLI, STM32CubeProgrammer, ST-Link, DFU, and USB pass-through to WSL2 | Bash, PowerShell, WSL2, usbipd |
